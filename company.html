Company

Kordane Drives is a motor maker with one focus: electric traction.

We design, test and document traction motors for electric vehicle makers and drivetrain integrators. Our engineers work on electromagnetic design, thermal design and test, and support customer inverter teams during integration.

We publish full datasheets for every product so engineers can evaluate a motor before they contact us.
