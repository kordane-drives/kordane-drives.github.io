Technology

How our motors are built and what makes them efficient.

Interior permanent magnets

Magnets sit inside the rotor core. This gives a strong reluctance torque on top of the magnet torque, so the motor holds its power across a wide speed range.

Field weakening

At high speed the inverter reduces the magnet field. The motor keeps running well beyond its base speed without exceeding the DC-link voltage.

Thermal design

A water-glycol jacket and temperature sensors in the winding let the controller derate smoothly instead of tripping.

Built to be controlled.

Each motor has a resolver for rotor position and NTC sensors for temperature. Current-loop and protection settings are listed in the datasheet so your control team can start from known values.
