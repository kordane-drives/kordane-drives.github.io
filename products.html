EPM-240-8P

Traction motor, 150 kW continuous

An 8-pole interior permanent-magnet synchronous motor for 800 V drivetrains. It delivers 150 kW continuously and 220 kW at peak, with a maximum speed of 12 000 r/min. Water-glycol cooled and sealed to IP67.
